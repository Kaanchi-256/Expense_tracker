Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, as well as the expense records, categories, amounts, and dates you enter while using the Service. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and general usage activity, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide core functionality — storing and displaying your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and keeping your account secure. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to any third party.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting or font delivery. These providers only process the minimum data necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense records with any third party for marketing purposes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website. We're happy to help with any concerns about your data or account.
